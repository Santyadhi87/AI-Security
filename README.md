- AI/ML fundamentals — how models work, neural networks, LLMs, and their role in cybersecurity

- Secure AI systems — AI threat modelling, reconnaissance, and securing AI infrastructure
- AI supply chain security — model artefact integrity, dependency risks, and supply chain attacks
